# Tooling friction and worker management survey

Complete every three days. Keep each answer concrete and brief; write `none` when
nothing applies. Send one controller-mail answer to the registered Goal Flight
controller, which aggregates responses across controllers. Do not post the answers
as wake mail or include credentials, private prompts, or customer data.

1. What were you trying to accomplish, and what slowed or blocked the work?
2. Which tool, command, document, or permission caused the most friction?
3. What workaround, repeated investigation, or rework did that create?
4. What one change would make the next similar task faster or clearer?
5. Was the task context complete and current? What was missing or unnecessary?
6. Was the worker and route a good match for the task? Why?
7. Did workers start, resume, and finish reliably? Note any stalls, duplicate work,
   unclear ownership, or recovery gaps.
8. Was available worker capacity sufficient? Note waits, contention, or unused
   capacity that changed your plan.
9. Were worker results and independent reviews timely and actionable?
10. What change to worker prompts, lifecycle, or coordination would remove the most
    recurring management effort?

Return the answers with the controller label and survey date. The Goal Flight
controller owns cross-controller aggregation and any follow-up tasks.
